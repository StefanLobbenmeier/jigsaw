## The Great Wave off Kanagawa

- Author: anonymous reprint after Katsushika Hokusai (1760–1849); Commons identifies this as an Adachi reprint from the Shōwa period. It is not presented as an original impression.
- Digitization/source: Library of Congress.
- [Source and licensing record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Great_Wave_off_Kanagawa.jpg), reviewed revision 1122179216.
- [Original source linked by Commons](https://hdl.loc.gov/loc.pnp/jpd.02018).
- Rights basis recorded on Commons: expired Hokusai copyright, pre-1931 US publication, faithful 2D reproduction; Commons PD-Art and Public Domain Mark 1.0.
- Bundled `great-wave.jpg`: 1280 × 903 JPEG thumbnail generated by Wikimedia, downloaded without further pixel edits. The app center crops it at runtime.
- Download: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f7/Great_Wave_off_Kanagawa.jpg/1280px-Great_Wave_off_Kanagawa.jpg
